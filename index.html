ABOUT CTS

The Premier Technology
Conference for the Community of Faith

The Church & Technology Summit, being held at the Experia Summit, equips churches to lead with excellence, relevance, and vision in a rapidly changing digital world. This summit focuses on the practical intersection of ministry, leadership, and technology—helping churches steward tools that enhance worship, operations, communication, and discipleship without compromising spiritual integrity.

From executive leadership and administration to worship, production, IT, and NextGen engagement, this summit empowers churches of every size to operate more effectively, communicate more clearly, and prepare for the future. It is designed for churches that understand technology is no longer optional—it’s missional.

TRACKS

Church & Technology Summit Tracks

Church Administration & Operations

This track focuses on the systems that support ministry effectiveness—finance, HR, governance, compliance, and church management platforms. Attendees learn how to improve efficiency and stewardship through smart technology adoption

Creatives & Communications

Designed for media and communications teams, this track explores digital storytelling, branding, livestreaming, social media, and content strategy. It also highlights how AI and emerging tools can amplify creativity and reach.

IT, Cybersecurity & Emerging Technology

This track equips IT leaders to build secure, scalable, and future-ready church technology environments. Topics include infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud systems, data protection, and emerging tech trends impacting ministry.

Executive Leadership & Strategy

Focused on vision and governance, this track helps senior leaders navigate digital transformation, organizational change, and innovation. Sessions emphasize strategic leadership, alignment, and long-term sustainability.

Production Engineering & Experience Design

This track dives into the technical side of immersive worship and events—audio, lighting, video, LED walls, and experience orchestration. Attendees learn how to design environments that enhance engagement and encounter.

Worship Technology & Experience

Built for worship leaders and tech teams, this track covers worship systems, rehearsal workflows, technology integration, and tools that support excellence without distraction—keeping worship authentic and Spirit-led.

NextGen & Digital Discipleship

Focused on engaging Gen Z and Gen Alpha, this track equips youth pastors and leaders to disciple digital natives. Topics include online culture, social media, gaming, AI awareness, mental health, and building future-ready youth ministries.
